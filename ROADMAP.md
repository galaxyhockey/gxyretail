# Roadmap

## Shipped

- **Ten courses, 50 units** in four stages, from how retail makes money to strategy, ending in a capstone store teardown. Each unit includes the math, a sourced or clearly hypothetical case, a lab, and a field prompt.
- **Question of the day.** Drawn from the units you have opened. Missed questions come back after two days, then three.
- **Review deck.** Leitner spaced repetition over check questions and vocabulary.
- **18 labs**, plus an 8-week pricing simulation.
- **Assignments.** Numeric answers are auto-checked, written answers are compared against a model answer, and every course has a field assignment.
- **Field kit and notebook.** Tally counter, queue timer, price audit, unit price, and Markdown export.
- **Own theme.** "Shelf and tag", with light and dark modes and a shelf-tag icon.
- **Standalone repository.** Moved out of `gxylycium/retail/`, with its own config, lock generator and rules.

## Next

1. **Seasonal sets.** Add a holiday/back-to-school unit pack that the daily question favors in season.
2. **Photo notes.** Optionally attach a photo to a field observation, stored locally only.
3. **Store comparisons.** Chart price-audit indexes over time for the same pair of stores.
