About this school

Endcap is a field school built around one profession: retail management. It is a sibling of Lyceum, the generalist school, and runs the same way: static files, an optional Firebase back end, no tracking, no accounts you do not control.

On sources. The lessons, cases and exercises are original writing. They are grounded in Lumen Learning’s open Retail Management (the primary text), OpenStax and Saylor marketing texts, U.S. government data (Census, BLS, USDA, FTC, NIST), company filings, and peer-reviewed research, all linked in the Library. Factual claims about real companies come from those sources; hypothetical scenarios are always labelled. Where a famous retail “fact” is shaky — the jam study, beer and diapers — the lesson says so.

On numbers. Industry figures (margins, e-commerce share, company results) are dated to their source. Treat them as a snapshot and check the source for the latest.
